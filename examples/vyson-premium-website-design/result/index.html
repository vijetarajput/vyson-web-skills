Approach

We draw every room twice: once for the light, and once for the person who will live in it.

Light first
We study where the sun falls before we decide where a wall goes.
Fewer, better materials
A short list of materials, chosen to age well and repeated throughout the house.
Built to be repaired
Fixings you can reach, parts you can replace, and drawings you can hand to the next owner.

Process

From first conversation to the keys, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We visit the site, meet the household and write a short brief you can read in five minutes.

  2. 02

    Draw

    Sketches, plans and models until the rooms feel right, with a cost range at every stage.

  3. 03

    Build

    We check the work on site every week and answer builders’ questions quickly.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    A care guide, a set of as-built drawings and a visit after the first winter.
