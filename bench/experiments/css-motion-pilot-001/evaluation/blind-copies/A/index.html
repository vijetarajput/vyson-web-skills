Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Listen and survey

    We spend time with you and the site, learning how you live or work and what the place already offers.

  2. 02

    Sketch together

    Simple drawings and cardboard models let you react early, when changes are cheap and ideas are loose.

  3. 03

    Detail and approve

    We prepare clear drawings, handle planning approvals and agree materials and budgets before building starts.

  4. 04

    Build and hand over

    We visit site every week, work with your builder and stay involved until the keys are in your hand.
