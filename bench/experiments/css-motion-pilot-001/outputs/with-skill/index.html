How we work

A clear path from first conversation to the day you move in.

  1. Listen and visit

    We walk the site with you, learn how you live or work, and agree on goals and budget.

  2. Sketch and test

    Simple drawings and cardboard models let you react early, before details get expensive.

  3. Refine and document

    We settle materials, prepare permit drawings and clear plans that builders can price with confidence.

  4. Build and hand over

    We visit the site regularly, solve problems with the builder and walk you through the finished space.
