# Code of Conduct

VYSON Web Skills is open to developers of every experience level, including complete beginners. We want everyone to be able to ask questions and contribute without fear.

## Our standard

**Do:**
- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give specific, kind, useful feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

**Do not:**
- Harass, insult, or demean anyone, or make personal attacks.
- Post discriminatory or sexually explicit content.
- Share other people's private information.
- Spam or promote unrelated products.
- Submit work that copies someone else's without permission or attribution.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules. Serious or repeated problems may result in a permanent ban.
